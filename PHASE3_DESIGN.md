# monday.com Operating Plan: Phase 3 design
Management question: If growth decelerates faster than guided after the July 2026 restructuring, how should management adjust hiring and operating spend to protect margin and cash generation without damaging long-term growth?
Scope: quarterly plan, Q3 2026 through Q4 2027, with FY2026 and FY2027 totals. Cases: Base (the central estimate, NOT a conservative one) and one Downside (faster growth slowdown plus net dollar retention weakening toward about 105%). Q3 2026 base = the locked Q3 call midpoint (revenue $378.4M, non-GAAP operating income $69.6M); do not re-derive it. Monthly phasing is a later derived view and is labeled Assumed.
Labels: Sourced / Derived / Assumed on every cell. Every assumption carries: current level, forecast path, rationale, evidence row IDs, sensitivity.
Sheets: README and Definitions; Historical_Drivers (copied from the analysis workbook with row IDs); Assumptions; Revenue_Build; Headcount_Opex; PnL (non-GAAP with a GAAP bridge for SBC and restructuring); Cash_FCF; Scenarios (Base vs Downside with a bridge); Recommendation; Checks; Scorecard (blank, for November 9).
Revenue: candidate methods are (A) existing-customer growth implied by NDR plus a new-customer contribution derived as a residual, and (B) a sequential-growth path anchored to guidance and the historical deceleration. Choose by backtest, not preference.
Expenses: non-GAAP R&D, S&M, G&A as percent of revenue; S&M uses only post-2025 commission-change quarters for ratios. Headcount is not disclosed quarterly: carry an implied-headcount memo line (expense divided by an Assumed fully loaded cost per head, with sensitivity) used only to express the hiring decision in heads.
Restructuring: excluded from non-GAAP; the severance cash cost is an explicit Assumed cash item; flag that whether the FY FCF guide includes it is unresolved.
Cash: free cash flow = non-GAAP operating income x a seasonal conversion from history, plus only those items the evidence supports; buybacks assumed none (authorization exhausted), flagged. Q3 FCF was a NO CALL; the plan may carry a labeled low-confidence figure tied to the FY guide.
Recommendation: must be derived from the model outputs, expressed as trigger metrics and actions (for example, if NDR falls below X for two quarters, do Y), with the cost in growth foregone and the margin and cash preserved. Not written in advance.
Review gates before any freeze: formula logic, circularity, hidden hardcodes, unsupported assumptions, sign conventions, reconciliation, and whether the recommendation follows from the model.
Out of scope: the website, the 13-week liquidity module, interactive controls.
