monday.com Announces Third Quarter 2026 Results

 

Third quarter revenue of $377.5 million grew 19% year-over-year

Achieved record non-GAAP operating income and non-GAAP operating margin

AI products, including AI credits, agents and vibe, reached $36 million in AI ARR

 

New York / Tel Aviv, November 9, 2026 -- monday.com (NASDAQ: MNDY), the AI workspace that turns strategy into execution, at scale, today reported financial results for its third quarter ended September 30, 2026.

 

Management Commentary:

 

“Our third quarter showed both the strength of our foundation and the work ahead,” said monday.com co-founders and co-CEOs Roy Mann and Eran Zinman. “Our business is undergoing a fundamental evolution, and we took significant steps this quarter to become an AI-first company. While we remain highly confident in this strategic path, an initiative of this scope introduces near-term operational headwinds, and we are committed to executing with discipline as we work through them.”

 

“Our third quarter results reflect disciplined execution on profitability,” said Eliran Glazer, monday.com CFO. “Revenue grew 19% year-over-year to $377.5 million, and we delivered record non-GAAP operating income and operating margin. We are investing in enterprise go-to-market, product and AI, and our outlook reflects that balance.”

 

Third Quarter Fiscal 2026 Financial Highlights:

 

●Revenue was $377.5 million, an increase of 19% year-over-year.

●GAAP operating income was $31.2 million, compared to a loss of $2.4 million in the third quarter of 2025; GAAP operating margin was 8%, compared to negative 1% in the third quarter of 2025.

●Non-GAAP operating income was $89.4 million, compared to $47.5 million in the third quarter of 2025. Non-GAAP operating margin was 24%, compared to 15% in the third quarter of 2025.

●GAAP basic and diluted net income per share was $0.83 and $0.80 respectively, compared to GAAP basic and diluted net income per share of $0.25 in the third quarter of 2025; non-GAAP basic and diluted net income per share was $2.20 and $2.14, respectively, compared to non-GAAP basic and diluted net income per share of $1.20 and $1.16, respectively, in the third quarter of 2025.

●Net cash provided by operating activities was $20.4 million, with $63.0 million of adjusted free cash flow, compared to net cash provided by operating activities of $95.1 million with $92.3 million of adjusted free cash flow in the third quarter of 2025.

 

Recent Business Highlights:

 

●Net dollar retention rate was 108%.

●Net dollar retention rate for customers with more than 10 users was 112%.

●Net dollar retention rate for customers with more than $50,000 in ARR was 114%.

●Net dollar retention rate for customers with more than $100,000 in ARR was 113%.

●The number of paid customers with more than 10 users was 65,965, up 5% from 63,075 as of September 30, 2025.

●The number of paid customers with more than $50,000 in ARR was 5,038, up 26% from 3,993 as of September 30, 2025.

●The number of paid customers with more than $100,000 in ARR was 2,127, up 33% from 1,603 as of September 30, 2025.

●The number of paid customers with more than $500,000 in ARR was 123, up 58% from 78 as of September 30, 2025.

●Customers with more than 10 users now represent 83% of ARR, up from 81% as of September 30, 2025.

●Customers with more than $50,000 in ARR now represent 44% of ARR, up from 40% as of September 30, 2025.

●Customers with more than $100,000 in ARR now represent 31% of ARR, up from 27% as of September 30, 2025.

●Customers with more than $500,000 in ARR now represent 7% of ARR, up from 6% as of September 30, 2025.

●Total remaining performance obligations (RPOs) were $946 million, up 27% from $747 million as of September 30, 2025.

●Current remaining performance obligations (cRPOs) were $759 million, up 22% from $620 million as of September 30, 2025.

●Largest customer deployment grew to more than 87,000 seats.

●AI ARR reached $36 million in Q3; monday agents passed 6 million interactions in less than two quarters.

Definitions of Business Key Performance Indicators

 

Net Dollar Retention Rate

 

We calculate Net Dollar Retention Rate as of a period end by starting with the ARR from customers as of the 12 months prior to such period end (“Prior Period ARR”). We then calculate the ARR from these customers as of the current period end (“Current Period ARR”). The calculation of Current Period ARR includes any upsells, contraction and attrition. We then divide the total Current Period ARR by the total Prior Period ARR to arrive at the Net Dollar Retention Rate. For the trailing 12-month calculation, we take a weighted average of this calculation of our quarterly Net Dollar Retention Rate for the four quarters ending with the most recent quarter.

 

Annual Recurring Revenue

 

Annual Recurring Revenue (“ARR”) is defined to mean, as of the measurement date, the annualized value of our customer subscription plans assuming that any contract that expires during the next 12 months is renewed on its existing terms.

 

AI Annual Recurring Revenue

 

AI Annual Recurring Revenue (“AI ARR”) is a component of ARR and is defined to mean, as of the measurement date, the annualized value of our AI offerings, assuming that any contract that expires during the next 12 months is renewed on its existing terms. AI ARR consists of stand-alone AI products, AI credit packages sold for a subscription term, and the portion of subscription plans attributable to included AI credits, allocated based on the proportion of the list price of AI credits to the aggregate list prices of all products and AI credits in the arrangement. It excludes one-time purchases, overages and amounts not contracted on a recurring basis. The allocation is made solely for purposes of calculating AI ARR.

 

Remaining Performance Obligations

 

Remaining Performance Obligations (RPOs) are the aggregate amount of transaction price allocated to performance obligations that are unsatisfied or partially unsatisfied at the reporting date, including both deferred revenues and non-invoiced amounts expected to be billed and recognized in the future.

 

Current Remaining Performance Obligations

 

Current Remaining Performance Obligations (cRPOs) are the aggregate amount of transaction price allocated to performance obligations that are unsatisfied or partially unsatisfied at the reporting date, including both deferred revenues and non-invoiced amounts expected to be billed and recognized in the next 12 months.
